About

Biography

I am a Professor of Marketing at Birmingham Business School, University of Birmingham, where I serve as Head of the PhD Programme in the Department of Marketing. I hold a PhD in Marketing from the University of Strathclyde (2014).

My research sits at the intersection of marketing strategy, branding and emerging technologies. I study how customers engage with brands and interact with frontline employees, and how firms can use new technologies, such as blockchain and AI, to transform customer experiences. A core strand of my work examines how organisations, particularly in services, tourism and hospitality, build and communicate product and corporate brands to internal and external audiences.

My work appears in journals including the Journal of Business Research, Tourism Management, Annals of Tourism Research, British Journal of Management, European Journal of Marketing and Psychology & Marketing. I am Associate Editor of the Journal of Strategic Marketing, the Review of Marketing Science and the Journal of Service Theory and Practice, and serve on the editorial boards of several leading marketing and tourism journals.

I lead master's and MBA teaching in Branding and Marketing Management, including delivery on the University's Singapore campus, and supervise PhD and DBA candidates. I also write for business audiences in outlets such as The Conversation, Business Leader and Capital.gr.

Prospective PhD students. I welcome enquiries in branding, services, hospitality and tourism, digital assets and blockchain, and AI in services. Please email me with a short research proposal.

Research

Research themes

My research asks how firms, employees and customers create value together when services and technologies change. I mainly use scenario-based experiments with online panels, alongside surveys, configurational (fsQCA) and qualitative methods.

Frontline employees & service encounters

How customer incivility, customer threats and employee deviance affect frontline staff, and how organisational rewards and leadership help them cope.

Tourism Management (2020, 2024) · Annals of Tourism Research (2023)

Internal & employer branding

How internal marketing and internal market orientation turn employees into a source of brand equity.

British Journal of Management (2026) · Journal of Business Research (2021) · European Management Review

Hospitality, tourism & the sharing economy

Loyalty programmes, travel influencers, shared luxury services and in-home service consumption.

Annals of Tourism Research (2024) · Tourism Management (2025) · Journal of Business Research (2021, 2022, 2024)

AI, blockchain & digital assets

How AI announcements, AI recommendations, cryptocurrencies and NFTs reshape branding and customer relationships.
